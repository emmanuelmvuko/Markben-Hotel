SERVICES OFFERED

Luxurious Accommodations:

Experience unparalleled comfort and relaxation in our spacious and elegantly appointed guest rooms and suites. Each accommodation is meticulously designed to offer a harmonious blend of modern amenities and coastal charm, ensuring a restful stay for every guest.

Exquisite Dining:

Indulge your palate in a culinary journey like no other at our diverse dining establishments. From sumptuous seafood delicacies to international favorites and authentic Swahili cuisine, our talented chefs craft delectable dishes using the freshest local ingredients, promising a feast for the senses.

Event and Conference Facilities:

Host your next event or business gathering in style at our state-of-the-art event and conference facilities. Whether you’re planning a corporate meeting, wedding celebration, or social event, our versatile venues and expert event planners ensure seamless execution and unforgettable experiences for you and your guests.

Outdoor Activities and Excursions:

Immerse yourself in the natural beauty and adventure of Mombasa with our curated selection of outdoor activities and excursions. From exhilarating water sports and safari adventures to cultural tours and sightseeing, our concierge team is on hand to help you explore the best that the region has to offer.
